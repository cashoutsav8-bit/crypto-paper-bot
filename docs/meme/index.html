How the score works

Points out of 100:

  • Momentum (30): last hour's volume vs its own 24h pace, price change in the last hour, still rising in the last 5 minutes.
  • Buyers (12): share of trades that are buys, and unique buyers vs sellers (harder for bots to fake).
  • Chatter (25): mentions on /biz/, Reddit and news (news counts most), CoinGecko and GeckoTerminal trending lists, community takeovers, and whether mentions jumped vs earlier scans. Paid DexScreener boosts add a little: they're ads, but ads move memes.
  • Narrative (15): matches an upcoming catalyst (more points as the date gets closer), sits in a hot DexScreener narrative, or is named after something trending on Google.
  • Room to run (12) and liquidity (6): $100k-$1M market caps score highest because a 10-100x needs a small start; deeper liquidity means you can actually sell.

Then penalties: thin liquidity, launched in the last hour, already up 300%+ in a day, no sells (honeypot), dumping right now, and safety failures from RugCheck (Solana) or GoPlus (Ethereum, Base, BNB). 'Danger' means things like the dev can still print tokens, freeze your wallet, or block sells.

What it can't see: X/Twitter and Telegram, where a lot of meme coin hype starts. X has no free way for a bot to read it, so CoinGecko trending, DexScreener boosts and on-chain buying stand in for it. Base and Solana coins may be buyable in the Coinbase app's built-in DEX trading.
