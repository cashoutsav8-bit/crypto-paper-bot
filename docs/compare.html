C: position health

Why the open positions are down (-14.62 unrealized, live)

Biggest drags: BTC long -14.62 (coin -1.7% vs BTC -1.3%: BTC is the market)

C has no stops by design. It holds each coin while today's close is above the close 20 days earlier and sells at the first daily close that isn't. Positions are sized so each coin carries the same risk, so one coin moving against you is a small slice of the account.
